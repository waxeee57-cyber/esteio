# ESTEIO — Product

Portuguese industrial systems for scaffolding, temporary structures, and modular site buildings.

## Who it is for

Procurement managers, site engineers, and contractors specifying EN-compliant access, shoring, and modular units for Portuguese and Iberian worksites. They need load tables, lead times, and a quote path — not lifestyle copy.

## What it does

Sells three product families from Lisboa:

1. **Scaffolding** — façade frames, ring-lock modular, mobile towers, weather decks.
2. **Structural** — shoring, heavy-duty towers, formwork support, temporary bridges.
3. **Modular buildings** — site offices, welfare, workshops, multi-storey container systems.

The site is a sales instrument: catalogue download, specification tables, applications proof, WhatsApp and quotation form.

## Voice

Short. Technical. Confident. Numbers before adjectives. Never “innovative solutions”. Prefer “EN 12810 façade frames, 2.0 m bays, 6 kN/m².”
